7. Turn-on from blackout, final fade to black
==============================================================================
  blackout: sinks in their dead zones, V_out at the 3.5 V floor -> the array cannot conduct
  turn-on to 5 uA: V_out rises first (C_a lifts the cathode ~16 V), then the sink pulls it back down in 52.13 ms; the LED current rises only in the last ~2 V
  turn-on to 140 uA: V_out rises first (C_a lifts the cathode ~16 V), then the sink pulls it back down in 1.86 ms; the LED current rises only in the last ~2 V
  turn-on to 1000 uA: V_out rises first (C_a lifts the cathode ~16 V), then the sink pulls it back down in 0.26 ms; the LED current rises only in the last ~2 V
  turn-on to 100000 uA: V_out rises first (C_a lifts the cathode ~16 V), then the sink pulls it back down in 0.00 ms; the LED current rises only in the last ~2 V
  voltage-mode tail: one buck-DAC code = 9.8 mV -> 1.5 % LED current per code; 1 decade per 1.50 V of V_out
    natural decay limit at   5000 nA: e-fold in     2.1 ms
    natural decay limit at   1000 nA: e-fold in    10.3 ms
    natural decay limit at    100 nA: e-fold in   103.3 ms
    natural decay limit at     10 nA: e-fold in  1033.5 ms
  sink release from 5 uA without the tail: I(t) = C nNV_T/(t + 2.1 ms) -> 0.5 uA after 19 ms
